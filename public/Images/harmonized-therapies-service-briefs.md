## 7. HAVENING TECHNIQUES®
### *We can't always change what happened — but we can change how it lives in your body*

Some things stay with us long after they should have passed. The anxiety that arrives without warning. The memory that ambushes you. The feeling that no matter how much you process something, it's still there — sitting in your chest, your stomach, your throat.

Havening is different.

Developed by Dr Ronald Ruden, Havening Techniques® are a science-backed, evidence-informed psychosensory therapy. Through gentle touch applied to the arms, face and hands, Havening generates delta waves in the brain — the same waves produced in deep sleep — which help permanently de-link the emotional charge from distressing memories and experiences.

The result? What once felt unbearable becomes simply something that happened.

**Havening can help with:**
- Trauma and PTSD
- Anxiety, panic attacks and phobias
- Grief and loss
- Birth trauma
- Low self-worth and limiting beliefs
- Stress and overwhelm
- Anger, shame and guilt
- Chronic pain with an emotional component
- Insomnia and nightmares

**What a session looks like:**
You don't need to relive your experience in detail — Havening works even when we keep the content private. Sessions are gentle, conversational, and deeply safe. Many clients notice significant shifts in a single session.

Available in person in the Yarra Valley or via video call anywhere in Australia.

*"I walked out feeling so much better. I'd been holding on to a traumatic experience for a long time — I would highly recommend giving it a try."* — Havening client
